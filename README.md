This repository documents **practical, real-world sales techniques** I’ve used to manage and scale B2B sales pipelines in regulated environments.

It is not a theoretical playbook.
It reflects how deals actually move (or get stuck), how pipelines are built, and how sales activity translates into revenue forecasting.

The focus is on:

📈 structured opportunity management

🤝 pipeline discipline

🎯 forecasting accuracy

🚀 execution over theory

**Opportunity Creation & Data Discipline**

Each opportunity is created in the CRM with complete and structured information, including:

📌 Company name

📌 Address

📌 Tax ID

📌 Decision maker

📌 Email and phone

📌 Estimated annual turnover (USD)

This ensures:

📌 consistent qualification

📌 reliable forecasting

📌 clean downstream reporting

No opportunity enters the pipeline without minimum viable data.


**Sales Stages (1–9)**

Opportunities move through ***nine clearly defined stages***, each representing a concrete action or milestone.

**Stage 1 – Mapped**
Company identified and mapped. No outreach yet.

**Stage 2 – First Approach**
Initial contact made.

**Stage 3 – Presentation & Credit Check**
Presentation delivered and credit check initiated to evaluate a potential credit line.

**Stage 4 – Credit Approved**
Credit check passed. Required information requested to prepare a contract.

**Stage 5 – Contract Sent**
Draft contract sent to the customer.

**Stage 6 – Contract Signed & KYC**
Signed contract received and submitted for KYC review (UBO and supporting documentation).

**Stage 7 – Account Issued**
Customer account successfully issued.

**Stage 8 – First Statement Issued**
Customer receives their first statement.

**Stage 9 – First Payment**
Customer pays their first statement.

This structure ensures visibility across **commercial, risk, legal, and operational steps**, not just “sales activity.”


**Stage Aging & Stalled Opportunities**

Each sales stage has a maximum duration of 90 days.

If an opportunity does not progress to the next stage within that window:

❌ an alert is triggered

❌ the opportunity is flagged as stalled

❌ corrective action is required (advance, close-lost, or re-qualify)

This prevents pipeline inflation and forces honest deal management.


**Pipeline & Forecasting (Power BI)**

Using Power BI, I build a live pipeline view to:

✅ forecast revenue

✅ analyze stage conversion

✅ identify bottlenecks

✅ monitor pipeline aging

Forecasting is based on **actual stage progression**, not gut feeling.


**Core Metrics Tracked**

The following metrics are used to manage performance and pipeline health:

🧠 New opportunities

🧠 Stalled opportunities

🧠 Lost opportunities

🧠 Closing opportunities

🧠 Key actions needed & due dates

🧠 **Pipeline health**

0–90 days

90+ days

🧠**Conversion rate**

Target: **35%** (opportunities reaching Stage 7 – Account Issued)

🧠**Sales velocity**

Average sales cycle: **150 days**

These metrics are reviewed regularly to guide prioritization and resource allocation.


**Philosophy**

Pipeline size means nothing without movement

Forecasts must be defensible, not optimistic

Stalled deals are decisions avoided, not deals delayed

Process exists to support judgment — not replace it
